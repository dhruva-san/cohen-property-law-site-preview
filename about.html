About us

CPLG is a seasoned law firm that provides comprehensive real estate transactional services to individual and corporate clients nationwide. Our extensive experience handling complex commercial real estate transactions and our strong relationships with brokers, buyers, sellers, developers, real estate attorneys, and other real estate professionals allow us to keep a constant pulse on market trends and efficiently achieve the objectives of our clients.
