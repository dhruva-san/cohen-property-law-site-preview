Welcome to Cohen Property Law Group

CPLG is a real estate law firm that provides comprehensive real estate transactional services to individual and corporate clients nationwide.

Our extensive experience handling complex commercial real estate transactions and our strong relationships with brokers, buyers, sellers, developers, real estate attorneys, and other real estate professionals allow us to keep a constant pulse on market trends and efficiently achieve the objectives of our clients.

Our practice areas

Counsel across the life of a property, from acquisition and financing to leasing and management.

  • Acquisition, sale, and development of commercial real estateFrom pre-contract to disposition, we represent developers, investors, and other clients throughout the lifecycle of a real estate development project.
  • Real estate joint ventures and syndicationsHelping investors, syndicators, and partners navigate the legal complexities of these investment structures.
  • Commercial leasingDrafting, negotiating, and reviewing lease agreements for commercial tenants and landlords.
  • Real estate financingAdvising owners, developers, tenants, guarantors, and lenders on all aspects of commercial real estate financing.
  • Property managementLegal support for landlords and property managers on leasing issues, risk, and compliance.
